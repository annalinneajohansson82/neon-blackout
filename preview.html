Glow 8 colours, 4 strengths

Emission in darkness. Colours derive from palette tokens via color-mix, never from literals — a hardcoded rgba is a second source of colour that drifts silently. Strengths are uniform across all eight: perceived intensity is almost entirely the core, so a per-colour ceiling on the bloom is invisible, and capping the core removes the punch the system is built on. Intensity is controlled by prefers-contrast and --glow-enabled, which follow the viewer's own setting.
